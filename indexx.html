Life of a data scientist

As a data science student in Mzuni, my life is like running a race in which my laptop and I are perpetually on a low battery . Although 73% of us reportedly choose sleep over coding, my life is governed by stumbling blocks in terms of infrastructure. I am always in a rush to finish my complex assignments in the congested hostel where some people does not understand what it takes to be a data scientist, seeing them making unnecessary noise 24/7 . Between complex statistical concepts and the high-pressure environment of a relatively new department , the "five-hour sleep rule" is not a choice ; it is a necessity of trying to innovate in an environment in which the workload is high and resources are scarce. We joke that being a data scientist is a something everyone in our class enjoy but behind this joke there eis a reality of exhaustion from trying to prove that data scientists in Malawi can compete globally on nothing but energy and determination.
